# 🎵 SoundScope – Your Personalized Spotify Stats Dashboard  

**SoundScope** is a web app that analyzes your Spotify listening habits and provides insights into your music taste.  
View your top artists and tracks, and find out how unique your music taste is with the music popularity score!

## ✨ Features
- 🎧 **Spotify Login & Authentication**  
- 📊 **Top Artists & Tracks Analysis** (Short-term, Medium-term, Long-term)  
- ⭐ **Music Popularity Score** with dynamic visuals  
- 🎨 **Animated UI with GSAP & Flexbox**  

Try it for yourself [here](https://soundscope.onrender.com)!
